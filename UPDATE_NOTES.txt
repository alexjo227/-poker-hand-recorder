Poker Hand Recorder v5
New:
- Flop, Turn, and River fields share one compact row immediately after blinds/stacks.
- Card notation is rank-first, then suits: AJ5ccs.
- Suit picker buttons now use c, h, s, d instead of suit icons.
- Hands are still entered in BB, but saved/exported stacks and typed BB amounts are converted to chips.
- The second value in the blind level is used as the big blind (500/1k/1k = 1,000 chips per BB).
- Position-based postflop actor selection and manual override remain.
