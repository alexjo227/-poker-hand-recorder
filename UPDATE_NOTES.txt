Poker Hand Recorder v6
New:
- The entire five-card board uses one cumulative field immediately below the blinds and above effective stack.
- The board's rank and suit picker is directly below that field.
- Enter the full board as ranks first, then suits: AJK49ccsdh.
- Saved output automatically separates the first three cards as the flop, fourth as the turn, and fifth as the river.
- Card notation is rank-first, then suits: AJ5ccs.
- Suit picker buttons now use c, h, s, d instead of suit icons.
- Hands are still entered in BB, but saved/exported stacks and typed BB amounts are converted to chips.
- The second value in the blind level is used as the big blind (500/1k/1k = 1,000 chips per BB).
- Position-based postflop actor selection and manual override remain.
